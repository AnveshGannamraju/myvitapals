Vitamin A · Retinol & beta-carotene

The Night Watcher

Helps you see in the dark, keeps your skin and eyes healthy, and helps your body fight germs.

🧈 Fat-soluble

💪 Superpowers

  • Helps your eyes see in dim light
  • Keeps your skin and the lining of your nose and tummy healthy
  • Helps your immune system fight germs
  • Helps kids grow

🍽️ Find me in

🥕Carrots
🍠Sweet potato
🥬Spinach
🥭Mango
🎃Pumpkin
🥚Eggs

🪫 Running low? You might notice

  • Trouble seeing when it gets dark
  • Dry eyes
  • Getting sick more often

📏 How much do I need?

Adults need about 700–900 mcg a day. One baked sweet potato covers more than a whole day's worth!

🤓 Fun fact

The story goes that in World War II, Britain told everyone its pilots ate lots of carrots to see at night. The real secret was radar! Carrots do help your eyes, but they won't give you night vision.

⚠️ Heads-up

Too much vitamin A from supplements (not from carrots!) can hurt your liver and bones, and is risky during pregnancy. Beta-carotene from plants is safe. Eat a mountain of carrots and the worst that happens is your skin turns a little orange.

📚 Source: NIH Office of Dietary Supplements — Vitamin A fact sheet. Amounts are general daily needs for healthy adults. Last reviewed October 2026.
