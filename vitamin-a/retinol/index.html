Vitamin A · Preformed vitamin A

Retinol

Vitamin A that's ready to use the moment you eat it. It comes from animal foods.

🥚 Nickname: The Ready-Made

What makes it special

  • Comes from animal foods, mostly as retinyl esters, which your body turns into retinol
  • Your liver stores the extra for later
  • Your body turns it into retinal (for seeing in dim light) and retinoic acid (for growth and healthy skin)

Where to find it

🥚 Eggs🥛 Milk🧀 Cheese🐟 Fish🧈 Butter🥣 Fortified cereal

🏷️ Label decoder

Supplement labels usually say 'retinyl acetate' or 'retinyl palmitate'. Those are both preformed vitamin A.

🤓 Did you know?

Polar bear liver is so packed with vitamin A that eating it can make people seriously sick. Arctic explorers learned this the hard way!

⚠️ Heads-up

This is the form that can build up to harmful levels from high-dose supplements. That's especially risky during pregnancy. Some acne medicines are made from vitamin A too, and they need a doctor's care.

📏 How much Vitamin A do you need?

700–900 mcg a day for most adults. Upper limit: 3,000 mcg (retinol only).
