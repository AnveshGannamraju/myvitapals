Vitamin A · Carotenoids · Provitamin A carotenoids

The Plant Power

Colorful plant pigments that your body turns into vitamin A only when it needs it.

🧈 Fat-soluble

🔍 What makes it special

  • Beta-carotene is the big one. Alpha-carotene and beta-cryptoxanthin count too
  • Your gut converts them into vitamin A as needed
  • Some carotenoids, like lutein, zeaxanthin and lycopene, never become vitamin A, but they're still good for you

🍽️ Find me in

🥕Carrots
🍠Sweet potato
🎃Pumpkin
🥬Spinach
🍈Cantaloupe
🥭Mango

🏷️ Label decoder

Look for 'beta-carotene' or 'mixed carotenoids'. Some multivitamins use a mix of beta-carotene and retinyl esters.

🤓 Fun fact

Eat LOTS of carrots and your skin can turn a little orange. It's harmless and fades when you cut back. Basically, you become a carrot. Temporarily.

⚠️ Heads-up

In big studies, high-dose beta-carotene supplements raised lung cancer risk in smokers, so smokers should skip them. Beta-carotene from fruits and veggies is fine.

📏 How much do I need?

Adults need about 700–900 mcg a day. One baked sweet potato covers more than a whole day's worth!
