    Myth vs fact

    MythVitamin C prevents colds.
    FactFor most people it doesn't prevent colds, but regular intake may make them slightly shorter. Vitamin C →
    MythLiving somewhere sunny means you have enough.
    FactMany people in sunny places are low, due to indoor life, clothing, skin tone and pollution. Vitamin D →
    MythVegetarians get enough B12 from dairy.
    FactSome do, but many don't. Getting tested is the only way to know. Vitamin B12 →

    About & how we write

    VitaPals is written for adults aged 20 and over, for general education, not medical advice. Facts are based on the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements. Talk to a doctor, dietitian or pharmacist before starting a supplement, especially if you're pregnant, take medicines or have a health condition.
