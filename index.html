💧 Water-soluble

They dissolve in water, so your body flushes out extras. You need a steady supply from food, and too much from food is rarely a worry.

🧈 Fat-soluble

They're stored in your body's fat and liver, so they stick around. That's handy, but too much from supplements can build up and cause trouble.

🌈 Eat the rainbow

Most people get the vitamins they need from a mix of colorful foods: fruits, veggies, whole grains, beans, nuts, seeds and dairy. Different colors bring different pals!

🤔 Why A, B, C, D, E… then K?

F, G, H and friends were once on the list. Some turned out not to be vitamins at all, and others got renamed (vitamin H is now biotin, B7). K got its letter from the German word for clotting. Alphabet drama!

⚠️ More isn't always better

Supplements can help some people, but big doses can cause problems. Always check with a doctor or pharmacist first.
