    All 13 vitamins

    Tap any vitamin for foods, daily needs, who's at risk and supplement advice.

    💧 Water-soluble · your body doesn't store much, so you need them regularly

    Vitamin B1 · ThiamineTurns the food you eat into fuel your body can actually use, especially carbs.
    1.1–1.2 mg/day
    Vitamin B2 · RiboflavinHelps every cell make energy and keeps your skin, eyes and mouth in good shape.
    1.1–1.3 mg/day
    Vitamin B3 · NiacinHelps turn food into energy and keeps your skin, tummy and brain running smoothly.
    14–16 mg/day
    Vitamin B5 · Pantothenic acidHelps your body break down fats, carbs and protein, and helps make some hormones.
    5 mg/day
    Vitamin B6 · PyridoxineHelps your body use protein and cooks up brain chemicals that affect mood and sleep.
    1.3–1.7 mg/day
    Vitamin B7 · BiotinHelps turn food into energy, and is famous (maybe too famous) for hair, skin and nails.
    30 mcg/day
    Vitamin B9 · Folate (folic acid)Helps make and repair DNA and build brand-new cells, including red blood cells.
    400 mcg/day
    Vitamin B12 · CobalaminKeeps your nerves healthy and helps make red blood cells and DNA.
    2.4 mcg/day
    Vitamin C · Ascorbic acidBuilds collagen (your body's glue), helps heal cuts and keeps your immune system ready for action.
    75–90 mg/day

    🫒 Fat-soluble · stored in your body, so too much from supplements can build up

    Vitamin A · Retinol & beta-caroteneHelps you see in the dark, keeps your skin and eyes healthy, and helps your body fight germs.
    700–900 mcg/day
    Vitamin D · CalciferolHelps your body grab calcium from food to build strong bones and teeth.
    600–800 IU/day
    Vitamin E · TocopherolProtects your cells from damage, like a bodyguard for every cell in your body.
    15 mg/day
    Vitamin K · Phylloquinone & menaquinoneHelps your blood clot so cuts stop bleeding, and helps keep your bones strong.
    90–120 mcg/day

    Myth vs fact

    MythVitamin C prevents colds.
    FactFor most people it doesn't prevent colds, but regular intake may make them slightly shorter. Vitamin C →
    MythLiving somewhere sunny means you have enough.
    FactMany people in sunny places are low, due to indoor life, clothing, skin tone and pollution. Vitamin D →
    MythVegetarians get enough B12 from dairy.
    FactSome do, but many don't. Getting tested is the only way to know. Vitamin B12 →
