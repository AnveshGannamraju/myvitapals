Vitamin B1 · Thiamine

The Spark Plug

Turns the food you eat into fuel your body can actually use, especially carbs.

💧 Water-soluble

💪 Superpowers

  • Turns bread, rice and pasta into energy
  • Keeps your nerves sending messages properly
  • Supports a steady, happy heart

🍽️ Find me in

🌾Whole grains
🫘Beans & lentils
🌻Sunflower seeds
🥜Peanuts
🍞Fortified bread & cereal
🐟Fish

🪫 Running low? You might notice

  • Feeling tired and grumpy
  • Not feeling hungry
  • Tingly or weak legs

📏 How much do I need?

Adults need about 1.1–1.2 mg a day. A bowl of fortified cereal can cover a big chunk of it.

🤓 Fun fact

B1 was the first B vitamin ever discovered. Scientists noticed that people eating mostly polished white rice got sick, while people eating brown rice stayed healthy. The secret was hiding in the brown part!

⚠️ Heads-up

Drinking a lot of alcohol drains your B1. Your body would like a word.

📚 Source: NIH Office of Dietary Supplements — Vitamin B1 fact sheet. Amounts are general daily needs for healthy adults. Last reviewed October 2026.
