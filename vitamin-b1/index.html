Vitamin B1

Turns the food you eat into fuel your body can actually use, especially carbs.

Daily need (adults)1.1–1.2 mg a day
Upper limitNone set
Best foodsFortified cereal, beans, whole grains
Pay attention ifHeavy alcohol use
FoodsHow muchWho's at riskSigns of low levelsSupplementsFormsMythsFAQ

What it does

  • Turns bread, rice and pasta into energy
  • Keeps your nerves sending messages properly
  • Supports a steady, happy heart

🤓 Did you know?

B1 was the first B vitamin ever discovered. Scientists noticed that people eating mostly polished white rice got sick, while people eating brown rice stayed healthy. The secret was hiding in the brown part!

👩‍🍳 Kitchen tips

  • Thiamine leaks into cooking water. Use less water, or use the water in dal or soup.
  • Parboiled rice keeps more B1 than polished white rice.

How much do you need?

Men1.2 mg
Women1.1 mg
Pregnancy1.4 mg
Breastfeeding1.4 mg

Recommended daily amounts for adults, from the US Food and Nutrition Board.

ℹ️ Upper limit

No upper limit has been set. No harm has been reported from high intakes.

Who's most at risk of running low?

Heavy alcohol useAlcohol reduces B1 absorption. This is the most common cause of deficiency.
Older adultsThey often eat less and absorb less.
People with diabetesThey tend to lose more B1 in urine.
After weight-loss (bariatric) surgeryAbsorption drops after surgery.

Signs you might be low

  • Feeling tired and grumpy
  • Not feeling hungry
  • Tingly or weak legs

Severe deficiency (beriberi or Wernicke-Korsakoff syndrome) is a medical emergency, mostly seen with heavy drinking.

🩺 When to see a doctor

These signs have many possible causes. If they last more than a couple of weeks, see a doctor. A simple blood test can often check your levels. Don't guess with high-dose supplements.

Supplements: do you need one?

When it helps

Usually only needed for heavy drinkers or after bariatric surgery, under a doctor's care.

Choosing one

Thiamine (thiamine HCl or mononitrate) is the standard form. Benfotiamine is a lab-made version sold in some supplements.

💊 Medicine interactions

  • Furosemide (Lasix), a water pill, may lower B1 levels
  • Fluorouracil (5-FU), a cancer drug, can block B1 use

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about any supplements you take.

Forms of Vitamin B1

B1 has one main form in food, and your body turns it into an active 'spark' form.

  • ThiamineIn food

    The form found in food and most supplements.

  • Thiamine pyrophosphate (TPP)Active form

    The active form your body makes. It's the spark that helps turn carbs into energy.

  • BenfotiamineSupplements

    A lab-made form of thiamine found in some supplements.

Myths vs facts

MythB1 boosts energy if you feel tired.
FactB1 helps turn food into energy, but extra won't energize you unless you're actually low.

Frequently asked questions

Can vegetarians get enough B1?

Yes. Whole grains, beans, lentils, seeds and fortified cereals are all good sources.

Why is white rice 'enriched'?

Polishing rice removes the B1-rich outer layer. Enriched rice adds some back.
