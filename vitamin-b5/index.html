Pantothenic acid · Water-soluble

Vitamin B5

Helps your body break down fats, carbs and protein, and helps make some hormones.

🌍 Nickname: Mr. Everywhere
Daily need (adults)5 mg a day
Upper limitNone set
Best foodsMushrooms, sunflower seeds, chicken
Pay attention ifAlmost nobody
FoodsHow muchWho's at riskSigns of low levelsSupplementsFormsMythsFAQ

What it does

  • Releases energy from food
  • Helps build the fats your cells need
  • Helps make some important hormones

🤓 Did you know?

'Pantothen' is Greek for 'from everywhere'. B5 is in so many foods that running low is super rare.

👩‍🍳 Kitchen tips

  • Almost every food has some B5. Whole, less-processed foods have the most.

How much do you need?

Adults5 mg
Pregnancy6 mg
Breastfeeding7 mg

Recommended daily amounts for adults, from the US Food and Nutrition Board.

ℹ️ Upper limit

No upper limit has been set.

Who's most at risk of running low?

Almost nobodyDeficiency is extremely rare and only seen with severe malnutrition or a rare genetic condition.

Signs you might be low

  • Very rare! Almost everyone gets enough
  • Tiredness
  • A 'burning feet' feeling (only in extreme cases)

Low levels are so rare that symptoms are mostly known from experiments.

🩺 When to see a doctor

These signs have many possible causes. If they last more than a couple of weeks, see a doctor. A simple blood test can often check your levels. Don't guess with high-dose supplements.

Supplements: do you need one?

When it helps

Not needed. A varied diet covers it.

Choosing one

Calcium pantothenate is the usual supplement form.

💊 Medicine interactions

  • No clinically important medicine interactions are known

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about any supplements you take.

Forms of Vitamin B5

Pantothenic acid is the food form. Your body turns it into coenzyme A, a helper used all over your body.

  • Pantothenic acidIn food

    The form found naturally in food.

  • Calcium pantothenateSupplements

    The most common form in supplements.

  • PantethineSupplements

    Another supplement form.

  • Coenzyme AActive form

    The active form your body makes. It helps break down fats, carbs and protein.

  • PanthenolPersonal care

    Seen 'pro-vitamin B5' on a shampoo bottle? That's panthenol, used in hair and skin products.

Myths vs facts

MythB5 supplements cure acne.
FactThere isn't good evidence for this.

Frequently asked questions

Is panthenol in shampoo good for hair?

It helps hair feel softer and look shinier, but it doesn't change your body's B5 levels.
