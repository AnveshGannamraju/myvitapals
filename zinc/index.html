Zinc

Supports your immune system, helps wounds heal, and keeps your sense of taste and smell working.

Daily need (adults)8–11 mg a day
Upper limit40 mg
Best foodsOysters, beef, fortified cereal
Pay attention ifVegetarians and vegans
FoodsHow muchWho's at riskSigns of low levelsSupplementsFormsMythsFAQ

What it does

  • Supports your immune system
  • Helps wounds heal
  • Keeps taste and smell working
  • Helps make proteins and DNA

🤓 Did you know?

Three ounces of oysters have more than two days' worth of zinc.

👩‍🍳 Kitchen tips

  • Soak, sprout or ferment beans and grains (sprouts, idli, dosa). It lowers phytates, so you absorb more zinc.
  • If you take iron of 25 mg or more, take zinc at a different time.

How much do you need?

Men11 mg
Women8 mg
Pregnancy11 mg
Breastfeeding12 mg

Recommended daily amounts for adults, from the US Food and Nutrition Board.

⚠️ Upper limit

40 mg a day. Taking 50 mg or more for long periods can cause copper deficiency and weaken immunity.

Who's most at risk of running low?

Vegetarians and vegansIntake and absorption tend to be lower.
Pregnancy and breastfeedingNeeds rise.
Gut conditions or surgeryIBD, celiac disease or weight-loss surgery.
Heavy drinkingAlcohol lowers zinc absorption and raises losses.

Signs you might be low

  • Loss of appetite
  • Changes in taste or smell
  • Slow wound healing
  • Frequent infections or hair loss

Mild zinc deficiency is hard to spot with a blood test, so doctors look at your diet and symptoms too.

🩺 When to see a doctor

These signs have many possible causes. If they last more than a couple of weeks, see a doctor. A simple blood test can often check your levels. Don't guess with high-dose supplements.

Supplements: do you need one?

When it helps

Usually only if your diet is low in zinc, or your doctor advises it. Zinc lozenges started early may shorten a cold by about 2 days.

Choosing one

Labels list elemental zinc. Gluconate, sulfate, acetate and citrate are common. Stay under 40 mg a day unless your doctor says otherwise.

💊 Medicine interactions

  • Antibiotics (quinolones like ciprofloxacin, tetracyclines like doxycycline): take the antibiotic 2 hours before or 4–6 hours after zinc
  • Penicillamine (for arthritis or Wilson's disease): take it at least 1 hour apart from zinc
  • Thiazide water pills (like hydrochlorothiazide) increase zinc loss

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about any supplements you take.

Forms of Zinc

Zinc in food comes with protein. Supplements come in several salts.

  • Zinc in foodIn food

    Seafood, meat, dairy, seeds, beans and whole grains.

  • Zinc gluconate and acetateSupplements

    Common in lozenges and supplements.

  • Zinc sulfate, citrate, oxideSupplements

    Other supplement forms. Labels list elemental zinc.

Myths vs facts

MythZinc prevents colds.
FactA 2024 Cochrane review found little to no effect on preventing colds. Started early, lozenges may shorten one by about 2 days.
MythMore zinc means a stronger immune system.
FactToo much, 50 mg or more for long periods, can actually weaken immunity and cause copper deficiency.

Frequently asked questions

Do vegetarians need zinc supplements?

Not automatically. Dals, beans, seeds, whole grains and dairy all have zinc, and soaking or sprouting helps you absorb more. Ask your doctor if you're concerned.

What about zinc for eyesight?

In a large trial, a specific formula with 80 mg zinc plus vitamins lowered the risk of advanced macular degeneration by 25% in people at high risk. It's a medical regimen, not an everyday supplement.
