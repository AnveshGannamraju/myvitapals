About

Hi, we're VitaPals! 💊

Vitamins can sound like a science exam. We think they should feel more like meeting new friends.

What is this site?

VitaPals explains vitamins in plain, friendly language, with a little humor, for kids, parents and grandparents alike. Each vitamin gets one simple page: what it does, where to find it, signs you might be low, and a fun fact.

Where does the information come from?

Our facts and daily amounts are based on the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements fact sheets. Each page links to its source.

Is this medical advice?

Nope. VitaPals is for learning and curiosity. Everyone's body is different, so please talk to a doctor, dietitian or pharmacist before starting any supplement or changing your diet for a health reason.

What's next?

Minerals like iron, calcium and zinc are on our wish list. Stay tuned!
