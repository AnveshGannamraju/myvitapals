Vitamin C · Ascorbic acid

The Citrus Superhero

Builds collagen (your body's glue), helps heal cuts and keeps your immune system ready for action.

💧 Water-soluble

💪 Superpowers

  • Builds collagen, the glue for your skin, gums, blood vessels and bones
  • Helps cuts and scrapes heal
  • Supports your immune system
  • Helps your body soak up iron from plant foods

🍽️ Find me in

🍊Oranges
🫑Bell peppers
🥝Kiwi
🍓Strawberries
🥦Broccoli
🍅Tomatoes

🪫 Running low? You might notice

  • Bleeding or swollen gums
  • Bruising easily
  • Cuts that heal slowly
  • Feeling tired

📏 How much do I need?

Adults need about 75–90 mg a day (smokers need a bit more). One orange gets you most of the way. Half a cup of red bell pepper gets you all the way!

🤓 Fun fact

Most animals make their own vitamin C, but humans can't. Neither can guinea pigs or fruit bats. Old-time sailors got sick on long trips until they packed limes, which is how British sailors got the nickname 'limeys'.

⚠️ Heads-up

Very high doses from supplements can cause tummy aches and diarrhea, and may raise the chance of kidney stones in some people. Fruits and veggies? Always fine. And sorry: for most people, vitamin C won't stop a cold, though it may make it a little shorter.

📚 Source: NIH Office of Dietary Supplements — Vitamin C fact sheet. Amounts are general daily needs for healthy adults. Last reviewed October 2026.
