Vitamin E · Alpha, beta, gamma & delta

Tocotrienols

Four lesser-known forms of vitamin E, found mainly in certain oils and grains.

🌴 Nickname: The Cousins

What makes it special

  • Come in alpha, beta, gamma and delta forms, just like tocopherols
  • Shaped a little differently: their 'tail' is bendier
  • They don't count toward your daily vitamin E needs. Scientists are still studying what they do

Where to find it

🌴 Palm oil🍚 Rice bran oil🌾 Barley & oats

🏷️ Label decoder

Usually sold as 'tocotrienol complex', often made from palm or annatto.

🤓 Did you know?

Tocotrienols were first found in 1964, in rubber tree sap. That's more than 40 years after vitamin E was discovered. Fashionably late!

⚠️ Heads-up

Research on tocotrienol supplements is still early. Like other vitamin E supplements, they may affect bleeding, so check with a doctor first.

📏 How much Vitamin E do you need?

15 mg a day for most adults. Upper limit: 1,000 mg (supplements).
