Vitamin E · Tocopherols · Alpha, beta, gamma & delta

The Main Guards

Four forms of vitamin E, and alpha-tocopherol is the one your body keeps.

🧈 Fat-soluble

🔍 What makes it special

  • Come in alpha, beta, gamma and delta forms
  • Your liver holds on to alpha-tocopherol and sends it around your body. It breaks the others down faster
  • Only alpha-tocopherol counts toward your daily 15 mg

🍽️ Find me in

🌻Sunflower seeds & oil
🌰Almonds & hazelnuts
🥜Peanut butter
🥬Spinach
🌽Corn & soybean oil (gamma)
🥑Avocado

🏷️ Label decoder

'd-alpha-tocopherol' is natural. 'dl-alpha-tocopherol' is lab-made and only about half as strong per milligram. 'Mixed tocopherols' means all four.

🤓 Fun fact

Food makers add tocopherols to oils and snacks to stop them going stale. If you see it on an ingredient list, it's working as a natural preservative!

⚠️ Heads-up

High-dose vitamin E supplements can raise the risk of bleeding, especially for people on blood thinners. Nuts and seeds are totally fine.

📏 How much do I need?

Adults need about 15 mg a day. A small handful (1 ounce) of sunflower seeds gives you about half!
