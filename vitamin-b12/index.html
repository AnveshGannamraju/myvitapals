Vitamin B12 · Cobalamin

The Nerve Guardian

Keeps your nerves healthy and helps make red blood cells and DNA.

💧 Water-soluble

💪 Superpowers

  • Protects nerves and supports memory
  • Helps make red blood cells
  • Teams up with folate (B9) to build DNA

🍽️ Find me in

🥚Eggs
🧀Milk & cheese
🐟Fish
🍖Meat
🥛Fortified plant milks
🥣Fortified cereal

🪫 Running low? You might notice

  • Feeling very tired
  • Pins and needles in hands and feet
  • Memory or mood changes
  • Pale skin

📏 How much do I need?

Adults need just 2.4 mcg a day, which weighs less than a single grain of salt!

🤓 Fun fact

B12 is the only vitamin with a metal inside it: cobalt! And your liver can store several years' worth.

⚠️ Heads-up

Vegans, vegetarians and adults over 50 are more likely to run low. Plants don't make B12, so fortified foods or a supplement may be needed. Ask your doctor.

🧩 Meet the family: forms of vitamin B12

B12 has four forms. Two are active in your body, and two are mostly found in supplements and shots.

  • CyanocobalaminSupplements

    Lab-made and super stable, so it's the most common form in supplements and fortified foods. Your body converts it.

  • HydroxocobalaminSupplements

    Often used in B12 shots.

  • MethylcobalaminActive form

    An active form. It's the main one in your blood.

  • AdenosylcobalaminActive form

    The other active form. It works inside your cells' power plants (mitochondria).
