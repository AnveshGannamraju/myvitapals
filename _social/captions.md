- Every caption ends with something to do: comment, save, share or tag. Those actions help the post reach more people.
- Hashtags: Instagram allows max 5 per post or Reel (since Dec 2025). Every post uses 2 anchors (#myvitapals #nutritionfacts) plus 3 topic tags. Paste them at the end of the caption.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour.

## 02-vitamin-d-sunny
Lots of sun and still low on vitamin D? You're not alone. ☀️

Sunlight alone doesn't guarantee enough:
🏢 Indoor jobs
🧥 Covered clothing
🌫️ Pollution
🤎 Darker skin makes less from the same sun

Adults need 600–800 IU a day.
Few foods have much, so most people rely on sun, fortified foods or a supplement.
But more isn't better: the upper limit is 4,000 IU.

🩺 In a risk group? Ask your doctor about a blood test.

## 03-b12-vegetarians
Vegetarian? This is the one vitamin to watch. 🌱

Plants don't make B12. Not spinach, not spirulina, not fermented foods.

Your best vegetarian sources:
🥛 Milk
🥣 Curd / yogurt
🧀 Cheese
🥚 Eggs (if you eat them)

Dairy helps, but many vegetarians still run low.
🩺 A simple blood test tells you where you stand.

📤 Tag a vegetarian friend who needs to see this.

## 04-iron-spinach
Sorry, Popeye. Spinach isn't the iron king. 🥬

½ cup of cooked spinach has 3 mg of iron, the same as ½ cup of lentils.
And your body absorbs iron from plants less well.

3 easy fixes 👇
🍋 Squeeze lemon on your dal
☕ Have chai between meals, not with them
🩸 Always tired? Get a blood test before you take iron

## 05-vitamin-c-colds
Does vitamin C stop colds? Short answer: not really. 🤧

For most people, it doesn't prevent colds.
Regular intake may make them slightly shorter. That's it.

You need 75–90 mg a day. One orange gets you most of the way 🍊
Amla, guava and mosambi are great too.

⚠️ Megadoses over 2,000 mg a day can upset your stomach.

📤 Send this to the friend who takes a vitamin C tablet every time they sneeze.

## 06-calcium-curd
Curd + milk = more than half your day's calcium. 🥣🥛

No pills needed for most people.

3 tips 👇
⏱️ Spread it out. Your body absorbs 500 mg or less at a time best.
☀️ Vitamin D helps you absorb calcium.
🌾 No dairy? Try ragi, til and calcium-set tofu.

## 07-upper-limits
More isn't always better. ⚠️

Most vitamins and minerals have a daily upper limit.
It's easy to go over without noticing: a multivitamin + single pills + fortified foods all add up.

Some surprises:
⚡ Long-term high-dose B6 can damage nerves
☀️ Vitamin D tops out at 4,000 IU a day
🩸 Iron tops out at 45 mg a day

📌 Save this for the next time you're in the supplement aisle.
📤 Share it with your family's "supplement person."

## 08-medicine-interactions
Your medicine and your vitamins may not get along. 💊

4 common pairs:
💊 Metformin lowers B12
🔥 Acid reducers lower B12 and iron
🦋 Thyroid pills: take them 4 hours apart from iron or calcium
☀️ Steroids lower vitamin D

🗣️ Tell your pharmacist about every supplement you take.
🚫 Never stop or change a medicine on your own.

📤 Share this with parents or grandparents on daily meds.

#myvitapals #nutritionfacts #supplements #medicationsafety #vitamins

## 09-magnesium-rice
Brown rice has 4× the magnesium of white rice. 🍚

Same ½ cup, cooked:
🟤 Brown rice: 42 mg
⚪ White rice: 10 mg

Refining strips most of it out.

Easy swaps 👇
🌾 Whole wheat atta, bajra, jowar
🎃 A handful of pumpkin seeds (156 mg!)
🥜 Peanuts and other nuts
