    The B vitamins

    Eight vitamins that work as a team to turn food into energy, keep nerves healthy and build blood cells. Your body doesn't store most of them, so you need a steady supply from food.

    All 8 at a glance

    VitaminDaily needBest foods
    B1 · ThiamineTurns the food you eat into fuel your body can actually use, especially carbs.1.1–1.2 mgFortified cereal, beans, whole grainsIndia: Whole wheat atta (roti, chapati)
    B2 · RiboflavinHelps every cell make energy and keeps your skin, eyes and mouth in good shape.1.1–1.3 mgDairy, fortified oats, almondsIndia: Milk & curd (dahi)
    B3 · NiacinHelps turn food into energy and keeps your skin, gut and brain running smoothly.14–16 mgChicken, fish, peanutsIndia: Peanuts (moongphali)
    B5 · Pantothenic acidHelps your body break down fats, carbs and protein, and helps make some hormones.5 mgMushrooms, sunflower seeds, chickenIndia: Mushrooms
    B6 · PyridoxineHelps your body use protein and cooks up brain chemicals that affect mood and sleep.1.3–1.7 mgChickpeas, fish, potatoesIndia: Chana & chole (chickpeas)
    B7 · BiotinHelps turn food into energy, and is best known, maybe too well known, for hair, skin and nails.30 mcgEggs, salmon, sunflower seedsIndia: Eggs (cooked)
    B9 · FolateHelps make and repair DNA and build brand-new cells, including red blood cells.400 mcgSpinach, beans, asparagusIndia: Palak & methi
    B12 · CobalaminKeeps your nerves healthy and helps make red blood cells and DNA.2.4 mcgFish, dairy, fortified foodsIndia: Milk, curd & buttermilk

    👀 The two to watch

    B12 (vegetarians, vegans, adults over 50, metformin or acid-reducer users) and folate (anyone who could become pregnant). The others are usually easy to get from food.

    🤔 Where are B4, B8, B10 and B11?

    They were once called B vitamins, but later research showed they weren't true vitamins, or they were the same as others. So the numbering has gaps.
