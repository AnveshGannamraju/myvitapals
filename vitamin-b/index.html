What the B-Team does for you

⚡

Energy from food

They turn breakfast into get-up-and-go.

🧠

Brain & nerves

They keep your body's wiring humming.

🩸

Healthy blood

B9 and B12 help build red blood cells.

😊

Mood & sleep

✨

Skin, hair & eyes

B2, B3 and B7 help you glow.

🧬

DNA & new cells

B9 and B12 are your body's builders.

👶

Healthy pregnancies

Folate helps babies grow strong.

🥗 Good news

Most people get enough B vitamins from a varied diet. Whole grains, beans, leafy greens, nuts, eggs and dairy are the all-stars.

🤔 Wait, where's B4? B8?

Some numbers were once called B vitamins, but scientists later found they weren't truly vitamins after all. They got kicked off the team. Harsh, but fair.

💧 Use it or lose it

B vitamins dissolve in water, so your body flushes out extras instead of storing them (B12 is the exception). That's why you need a steady supply from food.
