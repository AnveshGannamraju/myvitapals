Vitamin B7

Helps turn food into energy, and is famous (maybe too famous) for hair, skin and nails.

💇 Nickname: The Hair Hype
Daily need (adults)30 mcg a day
Upper limitNone set
Best foodsEggs, salmon, sunflower seeds
Pay attention ifHeavy alcohol use
FoodsHow muchWho's at riskSigns of low levelsSupplementsFormsMythsFAQ

What it does

  • Helps break down fats, carbs and protein
  • Supports healthy hair, skin and nails
  • Works with enzymes that build and burn fuel

🤓 Did you know?

Raw egg whites contain a protein that grabs biotin and won't let go. Cook your eggs and the problem disappears. Sorry, raw-egg smoothie fans!

How much do you need?

Adults30 mcg
Pregnancy30 mcg
Breastfeeding35 mcg

Recommended daily amounts for adults, from the US Food and Nutrition Board.

ℹ️ Upper limit

No upper limit has been set, but high doses can mess up lab test results.

Who's most at risk of running low?

Heavy alcohol useAlcohol lowers biotin absorption.
Pregnancy and breastfeedingBiotin levels can drop slightly.

Signs you might be low

  • Thinning hair
  • Red, scaly rash around eyes, nose or mouth
  • Brittle nails

Real deficiency is uncommon. Thinning hair has many other causes.

🩺 When to see a doctor

These signs have many possible causes. If they last more than a couple of weeks, see a doctor. A simple blood test can often check your levels. Don't guess with high-dose supplements.

Supplements: do you need one?

When it helps

Most people don't need it. There's little evidence that biotin supplements help hair or nails unless you're truly low.

Choosing one

If you take biotin, many hair, skin and nail products contain doses 100+ times your daily need.

💊 Medicine interactions

  • ⚠️ High-dose biotin can cause false lab results, including heart (troponin) and thyroid tests. Tell your doctor before any blood test.
  • Some seizure medicines (carbamazepine, phenytoin, phenobarbital, primidone) can lower biotin

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about any supplements you take.

Forms of Vitamin B7

Biotin keeps it simple. There's really just one form!

  • Biotin (D-biotin)In food

    The form in supplements and the form your body uses. Labels often say 'D-biotin'.

  • BiocytinIn food

    Biotin attached to protein in foods. Your body snips it free so it can use it.

Myths vs facts

MythBiotin makes hair grow faster.
FactIt only helps if you're deficient, which is rare.

Frequently asked questions

Should I stop biotin before a blood test?

Tell your doctor you take it. They may ask you to pause it before certain tests.
