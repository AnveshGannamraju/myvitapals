    Vitamin B7

    Helps turn food into energy, and is best known, maybe too well known, for hair, skin and nails.

    Vitamin B7 in 30 seconds

    Biotin (vitamin B7) helps enzymes break down fats, carbs and protein. Adults need 30 mcg a day, and cooked eggs, salmon, sunflower seeds, sweet potato and almonds supply it. Real deficiency is uncommon, and there's little evidence that biotin pills help hair or nails unless you're truly low. High doses can throw off some blood tests.

    What does vitamin B7 do?

    • Helps break down fats, carbs and protein
    • Supports healthy hair, skin and nails
    • Works with enzymes that build and burn fuel

    🤓 Did you know?

    Raw egg whites contain a protein that grabs biotin and won't let go. Cook your eggs and the problem disappears. Sorry, raw-egg smoothie fans.

    👩‍🍳 Kitchen tips

    • Cook your eggs. Raw egg whites contain a protein that blocks biotin.

    🌱 Vegetarian & vegan sources

    Egg-eating vegetarians get the most from cooked eggs (about 10 mcg each). Without eggs, lean on sunflower seeds, sweet potato, almonds and peanuts; milk, curd and oatmeal add small amounts.

    How much vitamin B7 do you need a day?

    Adults30 mcg
    Pregnancy30 mcg
    Breastfeeding35 mcg

    Recommended daily amounts for adults, from the US Food and Nutrition Board.

    ℹ️ Upper limit

    No upper limit has been set, but high doses can mess up lab test results.

    Who might run low on vitamin B7?

    Heavy alcohol useAlcohol lowers biotin absorption.
    Pregnancy and breastfeedingBiotin levels can drop slightly.

    Signs of low vitamin B7

    • Thinning hair
    • Red, scaly rash around eyes, nose or mouth
    • Brittle nails

    Real deficiency is uncommon. Thinning hair has many other causes.

    🩺 When to see a doctor

    These signs have many possible causes. If they last more than a couple of weeks, see a doctor. Assessment depends on diet, symptoms and history; laboratory tests are useful for some nutrients. Don't guess with high-dose supplements.

    Do you need a vitamin B7 supplement?

    When it helps

    Most people don't need it. There's little evidence that biotin supplements help hair or nails unless you're truly low.

    Choosing one

    If you take biotin, many hair, skin and nail products contain doses 100+ times your daily need.

    💊 Medicine interactions

    • ⚠️ High-dose biotin can cause false lab results, including heart (troponin) and thyroid tests. Tell your doctor before any blood test.
    • Some seizure medicines (carbamazepine, phenytoin, phenobarbital, primidone) can lower biotin

    Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about any supplements you take.

    Forms of Vitamin B7

    Biotin keeps it simple. There's really just one form!

    • Biotin (D-biotin)In food

      The form in supplements and the form your body uses. Labels often say 'D-biotin'.

    • BiocytinIn food

      Biotin attached to protein in foods. Your body snips it free so it can use it.

    Vitamin B7 myths vs facts

    MythBiotin makes hair grow faster.
    FactIt only helps if you're deficient, which is rare.

    Vitamin B7: frequently asked questions

    Does biotin help hair growth?

    Only if you are truly deficient, which is rare. There's little evidence that biotin supplements help hair or nails in healthy people. Hair thinning has many causes, so see a doctor if it persists.

    Can vegetarians get enough biotin?

    Yes. Cooked eggs for egg-eaters, plus sunflower seeds, sweet potato, almonds and peanuts. Dairy and oats add a little.

    Should I stop biotin before a blood test?

    Tell your doctor you take it. They may ask you to pause it before certain tests.
