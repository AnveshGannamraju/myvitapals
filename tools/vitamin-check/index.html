2-minute vitamin check

Answer a few questions about how you eat and live. We'll point out which vitamins deserve a closer look, and the foods that help. Your answers stay on your device.

1. How do you eat?
2. Your age
3. Are you pregnant, breastfeeding or planning a pregnancy?
4. How often are you outdoors in daylight (arms or face uncovered)?
5. Your skin tone
6. Leafy greens, dals or beans
7. Fresh fruit or raw veggies (oranges, guava, peppers, tomatoes…)
8. Nuts, seeds or nut butters
9. Do you smoke, or have several alcoholic drinks most days?
10. Do you take any of these medicines regularly?

Designed for adults aged 20 and over. This is an educational tool, not a diagnosis. Only a doctor and a blood test can tell you if you're low.

About & how we write

VitaPals is written for adults aged 20 and over, for general education, not medical advice. Facts are based on the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements. Talk to a doctor, dietitian or pharmacist before starting a supplement, especially if you're pregnant, take medicines or have a health condition.
