Vegetarian or vegan

A well-planned vegetarian diet can cover nearly all your vitamin needs. A few need extra attention, especially if you're vegan.

The vitamins to focus on

Vitamin B12The big one. B12 is naturally found only in animal foods. Vegans need fortified foods or a supplement. Vegetarians who eat dairy and eggs may still run low.
Vitamin DVery few plant foods have vitamin D. Look for fortified plant milks, UV-exposed mushrooms and sensible sunshine.
Vitamin B2If you skip dairy, you lose a main source. Fortified plant milks, almonds and mushrooms help.
Vitamin B9Easy win! Dals, beans and leafy greens are packed with folate.
Vitamin ANo problem. Your body makes vitamin A from carrots, pumpkin and greens. Add a little oil or ghee to absorb it.
IronPlant iron is absorbed less, so vegetarians need 1.8 times as much. Pair dals with vitamin C.
ZincZinc from beans and grains is absorbed less. Soaking and sprouting help.
Omega-3Flaxseed, chia or walnuts easily cover the plant omega-3 (ALA) you need.

A day that covers the tricky ones

BreakfastFortified cereal or oats with milk or fortified plant milk
LunchDal, palak sabzi, roti and a bowl of curd
SnackA handful of almonds and an orange or guava
DinnerChana masala, brown rice and a salad with lemon squeezed on top

💪 Don't forget protein

Vegetarians can easily get enough protein with a little planning. Build each meal around dal, beans, soy, paneer, curd or milk, and mix sources across the day. Protein: needs & best sources →

📌 Good to know

Beyond vitamins, vegetarians should also watch iron, zinc, calcium, iodine and omega-3 fats. We'll cover these in upcoming mineral guides.

🩺 Talk to your doctor about

  • Ask for a B12 blood test, especially if you've been vegetarian or vegan for years.
  • Ask about vitamin D if you get little sun.
  • If you're planning a pregnancy, discuss B12 and folic acid early.

Sources: NIH Office of Dietary Supplements fact sheets: Vitamin B12, Vitamin D, Vitamin B2, Iron, Zinc, Omega-3. Last reviewed October 2026. General education, not medical advice.
