Over 50

After 50, your body absorbs and makes some vitamins less efficiently, and many common medicines affect vitamin levels. A few small changes go a long way.

The vitamins to focus on

Vitamin B12Less stomach acid means less B12 absorbed from food. Experts advise adults over 50 to get most of their B12 from fortified foods or a supplement.
Vitamin DSkin makes less vitamin D with age, and after 70 your daily need rises to 800 IU.
Vitamin B6Needs rise slightly after 50: 1.7 mg for men and 1.5 mg for women.
Vitamin KImportant for blood clotting and bones. If you take warfarin, keep your intake steady.

📌 Good to know

Calcium also matters more after 50 for bone health. A mineral guide is coming soon.

🩺 Talk to your doctor about

  • Bring a list of every medicine and supplement to your next checkup. Metformin and acid reducers can lower B12.
  • Ask whether a B12 or vitamin D test makes sense for you.
  • Before starting any supplement, check for interactions with your medicines.
