Always tired?

Feeling tired all the time has many possible causes, including sleep, stress, thyroid problems, anemia, depression, diabetes and medications. Low vitamins are only one of them, but they're easy to check.

The vitamins to focus on

Vitamin B12Low B12 can cause tiredness, weakness, tingling and memory problems. It's common in vegetarians and adults over 50.
Vitamin B9Low folate can cause a type of anemia that makes you tired and short of breath.
Vitamin DLow vitamin D is linked with tiredness and muscle aches in some people.

What to ask your doctor about

Complete blood countTo check for anemia
B12 and folateOften tested together
Vitamin D25-hydroxyvitamin D
Iron & thyroidFerritin and TSH are common non-vitamin causes

📌 Good to know

Please don't self-treat with high-dose 'energy' supplements. Too much B6 can damage nerves, and supplements can hide the real cause.

🩺 Talk to your doctor about

  • See a doctor if tiredness lasts more than a few weeks or comes with weight loss, breathlessness, low mood or other symptoms.
  • Get urgent help for chest pain, fainting or severe breathlessness.

Sources: NIH Office of Dietary Supplements fact sheets: Vitamin B12, Vitamin B9, Vitamin D. Last reviewed October 2026. General education, not medical advice.
