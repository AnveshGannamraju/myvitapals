Vitamin D · Ergocalciferol

D2

The plant-and-fungus kind of vitamin D, made by mushrooms and yeast in UV light.

What makes it special

  • Made when mushrooms or yeast get UV light
  • Used in some supplements, prescription vitamin D and fortified foods
  • Your body turns it into the active form, just like D3

Where to find it

🍄 UV-exposed mushrooms🥛 Some fortified plant milks🥣 Some fortified cereals

🏷️ Label decoder

Labels say 'vitamin D2' or 'ergocalciferol'. It's always vegan.

🤓 Did you know?

Mushrooms are little sunbathers! Mushrooms grown or set out in UV light make extra vitamin D.

⚠️ Heads-up

Both kinds work, but studies suggest D3 raises blood vitamin D levels a bit more than D2. If a doctor prescribed vitamin D, follow their instructions.

📏 How much Vitamin D do you need?

600–800 IU a day for most adults. Upper limit: 4,000 IU.
