Vitamin D · Cholecalciferol

D3

The kind your skin makes from sunshine. It's also the most common kind in supplements.

What makes it special

  • Your skin makes it when sunlight (UVB rays) hits it
  • Found in animal foods like fatty fish and egg yolks
  • Your liver and kidneys turn it into the active form

Where to find it

☀️ Sunshine🐟 Salmon & trout🥚 Egg yolks🥛 Fortified milk🧀 Cheese (a little)🥣 Fortified cereal

🏷️ Label decoder

Labels say 'vitamin D3' or 'cholecalciferol'. Most D3 is made from lanolin (the natural grease in sheep's wool). Looking for vegan? Find D3 made from lichen.

🤓 Did you know?

Window glass blocks the UVB rays your skin needs, so sitting in a sunny window won't make vitamin D. Your skin also makes less in winter and as you get older.

⚠️ Heads-up

Too much from supplements can raise the calcium in your blood. Don't skip sunscreen to chase vitamin D. Ask a doctor before taking high doses.

📏 How much Vitamin D do you need?

600–800 IU a day for most adults. Upper limit: 4,000 IU.
