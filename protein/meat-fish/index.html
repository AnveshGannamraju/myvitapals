Protein source · Chicken, fish, seafood, red meat

Meat & fish

The most protein per bite, plus well-absorbed iron, zinc and vitamin B12.

What makes it special

  • Complete protein, and very easy to digest
  • Fish like salmon, sardines and mackerel bring omega-3 fats
  • Meat brings heme iron, the kind your body absorbs best
  • A palm-sized portion (3 oz, about 85 g) gives 19–26 g

🤓 Did you know?

Canned tuna and sardines are some of the cheapest complete proteins in the grocery store, and they don't need a fridge until you open them.

👍 Why it's worth eating

Fish twice a week covers protein and omega-3 at once. Grilling, baking and curries with less oil keep it lean.

⚠️ What to watch

Processed meats (bacon, sausage, ham, salami, hot dogs) are linked to colon cancer: 50 g a day raises the risk by about 18%. Red meat is a "probable" risk. Keep processed meat occasional and vary your protein.

💡 Easy win

Batch-cook chicken or fish on the weekend. A ready protein in the fridge makes weekday meals much easier.

📏 How much protein do you need?

About 0.8 g per kg of body weight a day for most adults: 46–56 g a day. More if you're over 65 or train hard.
