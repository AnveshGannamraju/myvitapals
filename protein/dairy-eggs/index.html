Dairy & eggs

Complete protein that's cheap, easy and vegetarian-friendly. Dairy brings calcium too.

What makes it special

  • Complete protein: all 9 essential amino acids
  • Dairy adds calcium, vitamin B12 and often vitamin D (if fortified)
  • Eggs add choline and vitamin B12, and need almost no cooking skill
  • Greek yogurt and paneer pack the most protein per serving

🤓 Did you know?

Greek yogurt and hung curd have more protein than regular curd for the same reason: straining removes the watery whey, which concentrates the protein. That whey is what goes into whey powder.

👍 Why it's worth eating

Easy protein at breakfast, the meal where most people fall short. Curd and buttermilk also bring live bacteria if they're fresh.

⚠️ What to watch

Sweetened yogurts and lassi can carry a lot of sugar. Full-fat vs low-fat dairy is still debated: the 2025–2030 US guidelines favor full-fat, while the American Heart Association still suggests low-fat. If you have high cholesterol, ask your doctor.

💡 Easy win

Make it a habit: a cup of Greek yogurt plus 2 eggs gets you past 30 g before lunch. A bowl of curd plus 2 eggs gets you about 20 g.

📏 How much protein do you need?

About 0.8 g per kg of body weight a day for most adults: 46–56 g a day. More if you're over 65 or train hard.
