Selenium

Helps protect your cells from damage and helps your thyroid hormones work.

Daily need (adults)55 mcg a day
Upper limit400 mcg
Best foodsBrazil nuts, tuna, sardines
Pay attention ifPeople on long-term dialysis
FoodsHow muchWho's at riskSigns of low levelsSupplementsFormsMythsFAQ

What it does

  • Part of enzymes that protect cells from damage
  • Helps your thyroid hormones work
  • Helps make DNA
  • Supports reproduction and immunity

🤓 Did you know?

Too much selenium has a giveaway sign: breath that smells like garlic.

👩‍🍳 Kitchen tips

  • You don't need Brazil nuts every day. Eggs, grains, dairy and fish usually cover it.

How much do you need?

Adults55 mcg
Pregnancy60 mcg
Breastfeeding70 mcg

Recommended daily amounts for adults, from the US Food and Nutrition Board.

⚠️ Upper limit

400 mcg a day. Too much causes hair and nail loss, a metallic taste and garlic breath.

Who's most at risk of running low?

People on long-term dialysisSelenium levels are often low.
People with HIVLevels are often low.
People in low-selenium regions eating mostly local plant foodsSoil levels drive what's in the food.

Signs you might be low

  • Rarely causes symptoms on its own
  • Can worsen thyroid problems when iodine is also low
  • Linked to a rare heart muscle disease in very low-selenium regions

Deficiency is rare in people who eat a mix of foods grown in different regions.

🩺 When to see a doctor

These signs have many possible causes. If they last more than a couple of weeks, see a doctor. A simple blood test can often check your levels. Don't guess with high-dose supplements.

Supplements: do you need one?

When it helps

Rarely needed. Supplements haven't been shown to prevent cancer, heart disease or memory loss in people who get enough.

Choosing one

If your doctor suggests one, keep your total under 400 mcg a day. In 2008, a mislabeled liquid supplement with about 200 times the stated amount sickened 201 people.

💊 Medicine interactions

  • Cisplatin (a chemotherapy drug) can lower selenium levels; the effect isn't clear, so ask your oncologist

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about any supplements you take.

Forms of Selenium

Selenium in food is mostly bound to protein. Supplements use a few forms.

  • SelenomethionineIn food

    The main form in grains, beans and nuts. Also used in supplements.

  • SelenocysteineIn food

    Found in animal foods.

  • Sodium selenite and selenateSupplements

    Inorganic forms used in supplements.

Myths vs facts

MythSelenium prevents cancer.
FactThe SELECT trial of 35,533 men found 200 mcg a day didn't lower prostate cancer risk.
MythSelenium fixes Hashimoto's.
FactIn some trials it lowered thyroid antibodies, but it didn't improve thyroid function or quality of life.

Frequently asked questions

How many Brazil nuts are safe?

One or two now and then is plenty. Eating 6–8 a day regularly puts you over the upper limit.

Should I take selenium for my thyroid?

Not routinely. Ask your doctor. The American Thyroid Association advises against it for pregnant women with thyroid antibodies.
