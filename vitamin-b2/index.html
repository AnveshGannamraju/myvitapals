Vitamin B2 · Riboflavin

The Glow-Getter

Helps every cell make energy and keeps your skin, eyes and mouth in good shape.

💧 Water-soluble

💪 Superpowers

  • Powers energy-making inside your cells
  • Helps keep skin and eyes healthy
  • Helps other B vitamins do their jobs (team player!)

🍽️ Find me in

🥛Milk
🥣Yogurt
🥚Eggs
🌰Almonds
🍄Mushrooms
🥬Spinach

🪫 Running low? You might notice

  • Cracks at the corners of your mouth
  • Sore, red tongue or throat
  • Itchy eyes that dislike bright light

📏 How much do I need?

Adults need about 1.1–1.3 mg a day. Dairy, eggs and greens make that easy.

🤓 Fun fact

Ever taken a multivitamin and then seen neon-yellow pee? Don't panic! That's B2 waving goodbye on its way out. Totally harmless.

🧠 Pro tip

B2 hates sunlight, which breaks it down. That's one reason milk often comes in cartons you can't see through.

📚 Source: NIH Office of Dietary Supplements — Vitamin B2 fact sheet. Amounts are general daily needs for healthy adults. Last reviewed October 2026.
