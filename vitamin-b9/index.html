Folate (folic acid) · Water-soluble

Vitamin B9

Helps make and repair DNA and build brand-new cells, including red blood cells.

🧱 Nickname: The Builder
Daily need (adults)400 mcg a day
Upper limit1,000 mcg (folic acid)
Best foodsSpinach, beans, asparagus
Pay attention ifAnyone who could become pregnant
FoodsHow muchWho's at riskSigns of low levelsSupplementsFormsMythsFAQ

What it does

  • Builds new, healthy cells
  • Helps make red blood cells
  • Very important before and during pregnancy for a baby's brain and spine

🤓 Did you know?

'Folate' comes from the Latin word 'folium', meaning leaf. The name is literally telling you to eat your greens!

How much do you need?

Adults400 mcg
Pregnancy600 mcg
Breastfeeding500 mcg
Could become pregnant400 mcg folic acid daily, plus food folate

Recommended daily amounts for adults, from the US Food and Nutrition Board.

⚠️ Upper limit

1,000 mcg a day of folic acid from supplements and fortified foods. There's no limit for natural folate in food.

Who's most at risk of running low?

Anyone who could become pregnantFolic acid before pregnancy helps prevent serious birth defects of the brain and spine.
Heavy alcohol useAlcohol blocks folate absorption.
Gut conditionsSuch as celiac disease or inflammatory bowel disease.
People with an MTHFR gene variantA common variant that can lower folate levels a little.

Signs you might be low

  • Tiredness and weakness
  • Sore tongue or mouth ulcers
  • Feeling irritable

Low folate can cause a type of anemia (large red blood cells). It looks a lot like B12 deficiency, so both are usually tested together.

🩺 When to see a doctor

These signs have many possible causes. If they last more than a couple of weeks, see a doctor. A simple blood test can often check your levels. Don't guess with high-dose supplements.

Supplements: do you need one?

When it helps

Recommended for anyone who could become pregnant: 400 mcg of folic acid daily, starting at least a month before pregnancy. Most others can get enough from food.

Choosing one

Folic acid is well studied and well absorbed. L-methylfolate is an alternative. Ask your doctor which suits you.

💊 Medicine interactions

  • Methotrexate (for arthritis or cancer): don't take folic acid unless your doctor advises
  • Seizure medicines (phenytoin, carbamazepine, valproate) can lower folate
  • Sulfasalazine (for ulcerative colitis) reduces folate absorption
  • Large amounts of folic acid can hide a B12 deficiency

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about any supplements you take.

Forms of Vitamin B9

Folate comes in a few forms. One is natural, one is made in a lab, and one is in between.

  • Food folateIn food

    The natural form in leafy greens, beans and oranges. Your body absorbs about half of it.

  • Folic acidSupplements

    The lab-made form in fortified bread, cereal and most supplements. Your body absorbs about 85% of it with food, even more on an empty stomach.

  • L-methylfolate (5-MTHF)Supplements

    The form that travels in your blood. It's also sold in some supplements.

Myths vs facts

MythYou only need folate once you know you're pregnant.
FactThe brain and spine form in the first weeks, often before you know. Start before.

Frequently asked questions

Folic acid or methylfolate?

Both raise folate levels. Folic acid is what most research on preventing birth defects used. Ask your doctor if you have an MTHFR variant.

Do men need folate?

Yes, everyone needs 400 mcg a day for healthy cells and blood.
